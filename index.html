Spoken English, plus English and all other subjects (sciences, Computer Science, Urdu, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies, Economics, Psychology, Sociology, Statistics and more) for Class 9, Class 10, 1st Year and 2nd Year.

Can I attend a demo class first?

Yes. Message us on Instagram to book a free demo class before you enroll.

Where will classes be held?

All classes are live and online. You can join from your phone or laptop.

Is Spoken English suitable for beginners?

Yes. The course starts from the basics, so no prior English is required.

Send us a message on Instagram and we will share the payment details and class information.
